Who We Are

Built on Dignity, Local Leadership & Radical Transparency.

Warm Hearts was founded to bridge urgent gaps facing African children—hunger during school hours, lack of learning tools, preventable childhood illnesses, and unsafe water sources—by funding African-led community solutions.
